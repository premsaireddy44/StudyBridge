Make every important student decision visible.

StudyBridge adds practical decision tools around university research: transparent evidence, course comparisons, cost planning, eligibility checks and application readiness.

Product direction: StudyBridge brings university information and student decision tools together in one clear experience.

StudyBridge feature set

Reusable product components for comparing, verifying and planning university applications.

1 · Money

True-cost planner

Net fee, scholarship, deposit, living costs, visa, IHS, flight and contingency with multiple budget scenarios.

2 · Fit

Course comparison

Modules, credits, assessments, technical depth, tools, contact hours, dissertation, accreditation and prerequisites.

3 · Outcomes

Employability view

Placement type, guarantee status, careers support, graduate outcomes, employers and evidence dates.

4 · Funding

Scholarship tracker

Amount, eligibility, deadline, automatic or competitive status, stacking rules and net fee.

5 · Visa

CAS readiness

Deposit, documents, financial evidence, TB, ATAS, credibility preparation and refund policy.

6 · Trust

Evidence centre

Source, academic year, checked date, conflict warning and “not published” labels for every field.
